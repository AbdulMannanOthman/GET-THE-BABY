Welcome to Get Yourself a Baby — 
a simple, modern online shopping website where customers can browse available T-shirts, 
view product images and prices, and add their favorite items to a shopping cart.

Get Yourself a Baby provides customers with an easy and convenient way to browse and shop for T-shirts online.

Customers can select a T-shirt and add it to their cart. The cart keeps track of:

Product name

Product image

Product price

Quantity

Total amount
